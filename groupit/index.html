The one rule

A patch is a run of touching cells of the same tone. A number says how many cells are in the patch it sits on — counting itself.

Patches cannot touch

Two patches of the same tone are never side by side. If they touched they would already be one patch — which is what makes the numbers bite.

Tap, or drag

Tap to mark Shadow, tap again to clear. Long-press (or right-click) for Light. Drag to lay a whole run at once.

Gems for every board

In the full game each board pays gems — more for a bigger, harder one — and one extra for a perfect run: no undo, no reset, no cell left on the wrong tone. Hints do not count against it.

Endless boards

Free play deals a fresh board every time — small or large, easy or hard, plain or with a rule that changes how the numbers count. There is a daily board too, the same for everybody. It is all in the app, and in your browser.

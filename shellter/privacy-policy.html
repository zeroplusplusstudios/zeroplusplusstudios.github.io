Privacy Policy for Shellter

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zero Plus Plus Studios ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Shellter (the "App").

Shellter does not collect any data. It has no user accounts and no sign-in, it never asks for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number, and it contains no advertising, no analytics service and no crash-reporting service. Nothing you do in the App is transmitted off your device: it contains no code that sends or receives data over a network, and it is fully playable offline.

1. Information we collect

None. We collect no personal information, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no location, no contacts, no photos and no usage analytics. No third-party SDK that would collect such data is present in the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your own game state locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes:

  • level progress, stars and unlocks;
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics and accessibility options.

This data stays on your device, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you uninstall the App. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

3. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell or transfer any information, because we do not collect any.

4. Advertising and purchases

This version of Shellter contains no advertisements and offers no in-app purchases. Any in-game currency or unlockable item is earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

5. Permissions

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, storage or your device's identifiers. The only permission it declares is network access, which it does not use to send or receive any of your information — the App has no networking code and works entirely offline.

6. Children's privacy

Shellter is suitable for a general audience. Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and the App's Google Play Data safety information will be updated to match before any such version is released.

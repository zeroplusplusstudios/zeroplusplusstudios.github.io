The one rule

A patch is a run of touching cells of the same tone. A number says how many cells are in the patch it sits on — counting itself.

Patches cannot touch

Two patches of the same tone are never side by side. If they touched they would already be one patch — which is what makes the numbers bite.

Tap, or drag

Tap to mark Shadow, tap again to clear. Long-press (or right-click) for Light. Drag to lay a whole run at once.

Three stars

One for solving it. One for leaving no cell on the wrong tone. One for needing no undo and no reset.
